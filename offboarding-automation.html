Completed Automation Project

Offboarding Automation Flow

Implemented a Microsoft Power Automate workflow to ensure every relevant system administrator received offboarding notifications, formally confirmed access removal, and remained visible in a centralized SharePoint tracking record until all assigned tasks were completed.

01 / Project Background

Closing the gaps in employee access removal.

The previous offboarding process relied heavily on manual communication. When HR initiated an employee offboarding, some system administrators did not consistently receive a notification to remove the user from the systems they managed.

This created a risk that access could remain active in systems outside the core IT environment, including databases, DevOps platforms, marketplace systems and other business applications.

02 / Business Challenge

Why the manual process was not sufficient.

Notification & Coordination Gaps

  • System administrators were not always notified when an employee was leaving the organization.
  • Access removal across multiple business systems was difficult to coordinate and track centrally.
  • Manual follow-up was required when administrators missed an offboarding request.

Completion & Audit Gaps

  • There was no reliable confirmation that every administrator had completed the required offboarding action.
  • Audit evidence was fragmented because confirmations were not stored in one structured location.
  • IT had limited centralized visibility into which systems were complete and which were still pending.
03 / Project Requirements

Requirements for the automated workflow.

  • Automatically notify all relevant system administrators when an offboarding request was initiated.
  • Require each administrator to confirm completion after removing the user from their assigned systems.
  • Store all responses in a centralized SharePoint list for tracking and audit purposes.
  • Automatically identify incomplete administrator tasks and issue recurring reminders.
  • Run reminder notifications at 8:00 AM and 4:00 PM every day until completion.
04 / Implemented Solution

Power Automate coordinated the full offboarding process.

The workflow started after IT received an offboarding ticket from HR. IT first removed the employee from IT-managed systems and submitted the initial Microsoft Form. Power Automate then created the offboarding tracking record in SharePoint and distributed notifications to administrators responsible for other systems.

Each administrator removed employee access from their assigned systems and submitted a Microsoft Form confirming completion. Every response updated the same SharePoint tracking record, giving IT a centralized status view.

Central Notification

Database, DevOps, marketplace and other system administrators received formal offboarding notifications from the workflow.

Completion Confirmation

Each system owner submitted a Microsoft Form after completing access removal for the systems under their responsibility.

Automated Follow-up

Scheduled flows checked for pending confirmations twice daily and sent reminders until all tasks were complete.

08 / SharePoint Tracking & Audit Trail

A centralized system of record.

The SharePoint list became the central system of record for the offboarding workflow. It captured the employee offboarding request and tracked the completion status of every participating administrator.

Because every system owner was required to submit a form after removing access, the workflow produced structured evidence of who completed each action and which tasks remained outstanding. This provided stronger audit evidence than relying on individual email confirmations.

09 / Automated Reminder Mechanism

Pending tasks could not silently disappear.

8:00 AM Check

A scheduled Power Automate process reviewed the SharePoint tracking data each morning and identified assigned administrators who had not completed their access-removal task.

4:00 PM Check

A second daily check repeated the validation in the afternoon. Pending administrators automatically received another reminder until the SharePoint record showed the task as complete.

10 / Technical Summary

From fragmented communication to auditable automation.

After HR initiated an offboarding ticket, IT removed access from systems under its direct control and submitted the initial Microsoft Form. Power Automate created the SharePoint tracking record and distributed offboarding notifications to the appropriate system administrators.

Database, DevOps, marketplace and other application administrators removed the employee from their respective systems and submitted completion forms. Each submission updated the SharePoint record. Scheduled Power Automate flows reviewed pending tasks at 8:00 AM and 4:00 PM every day and automatically reminded administrators who had not yet completed their actions.

The completed workflow reduced the risk of missed access removal, minimized manual follow-up, improved IT visibility and produced a centralized audit record for the entire offboarding process.
