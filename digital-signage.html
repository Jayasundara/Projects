Digital Signage System Implementation

A centralized on-premises digital signage platform for factory production and operations areas, designed to publish operational information, Power BI dashboards, videos, notices and other digital content while keeping administrative and playback traffic separated.

01 / Project Overview

Centralized content management for factory-facing displays.

The project created a centrally managed digital signage environment for operational and production information. Instead of maintaining individual screens manually, authorized administrators could manage screens, content profiles, schedules and publishing from one management platform.

The system supported multiple content types including Power BI dashboards, production and operations information, videos, special notices, announcements and other digital media.

03 / Objectives

What the project was designed to achieve.

Operational Visibility

Make production and operational information easily visible throughout factory workflows.

Central Publishing

Provide one platform for videos, notices, Power BI reports and other supported digital content.

Administrator Self-Service

Allow approved Operations administrators to create screens, manage content profiles, schedule content and publish updates.

Network Separation

Separate screen/playback traffic from management access to reduce unnecessary direct connectivity.

04 / Security & Access

Access was limited to what the system required.

  • Management console access was restricted to approved Operations Department Signage Administrators and IT Administrators.
  • The Screens Network was separated from the administrator management path.
  • Firewall rules were limited to connectivity required for signage operation, management and approved external services.
  • Power BI access used controlled outbound connectivity to app.powerbi.com and required Microsoft services.
  • Centralized administration reduced the need for direct manual management of individual factory screens.
05 / Delivered Capabilities

What was delivered.

Screen Management

Centralized creation, configuration and administration of factory display screens.

Content Management

Central management of videos, notices, operational content and other supported media.

Content Profiles

Reusable content profiles and playlists assigned to selected screens or screen groups.

Scheduling & Publishing

Authorized administrators could schedule and publish updates centrally.

Power BI Display

Factory screens were enabled to display Power BI reports using approved cloud connectivity.

Controlled Administration

Routine signage management moved to a governed, centralized process instead of screen-by-screen updates.
